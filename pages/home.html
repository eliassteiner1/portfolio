about me

I'm a mechanical engineer with an MSc from ETH Zürich, where I focused on robotics, machine learning and computer vision. I enjoy working where the physical and the digital meet: building systems that perceive their environment and act on it in the real world.

My academic projects have taken me from steering a magnetic catheter through a model of the spinal epidural space, to teaching a legged robot how to hand objects to people (work I co-authored for HRI 2026), to getting an omnidirectional hexacopter to autonomously insert a peg into an 8 mm hole mid-flight. Along the way, I've also applied these skills in industry and research settings: analyzing sensor data to localize methane leaks at Sensirion, testing cardiovascular implants at Biotronik, and building a multi-camera surgical dataset for 3D reconstruction at Balgrist.

Outside of code and robots, I like making things with my hands, whether that's machining engine models, 3D printing, drawing or sculpting. I play volleyball (and keep the books for my local club), surf and run whenever I can, and I've spent time travelling and living abroad, which is where most of my Spanish knowledge comes from.
