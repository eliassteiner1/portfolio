Hi, my name is Elias! I studied Mechanical Engineering at ETH Zürich where I graduated with an MSc. During my time at ETH I mostly focused on Robotics, Computer Vision and Machine Learning.

To me, the intersection between the physical and digital world has always been incredibly fascinating. Unique challenges tend to arise when we combine idealistic concepts from the digital realm with harsh realities and challenges from the physical world. In those exact situations it is critical to be strong in solving problems creatively and exploring options - something that I find absolutely captivating. Especially in the field of Robotics this combination of digital and physical happens all the time and is the main reason why I still find the topic so interesting.

This website serves as a collection of personal projects and academic work that I have accumulated over the years. Not everything featured here is strictly related to Robotics or my Academic interests?. Actually, some of the projects are a lot more focused on art, design and different fabrication skills. Nonetheless, the more I have worked on these different projects that initially arose/sparked from some inspiration I saw or some idea, the more I noticed that previously learned skills can transfer to other domains and can be unexpectedly helpful for a totally different project. Therefore, what ties all the showcased project here together, is that they show my approach to problem solving, as doing so creatively is ultimately just the combination of different ideas, patterns and technique - which are often more similar across totally different domains than we think.
