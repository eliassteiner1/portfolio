Hi, I'm Elias! I hold an MSc in Mechanical Engineering from ETH Zürich, where I mostly focused on Robotics, Computer Vision and Machine Learning.

I've always been fascinated by the intersection of the digital and physical worlds: ideas that work neatly in software tend to collide with the messy realities of hardware. I've had the chance to experience this first-hand on many occasions, most memorably while designing an autonomous drone controller for my Master's thesis. Finding solutions that work in both realms takes creativity and a willingness to explore options. Robotics, combining software and mechanics, sits right at this intersection, which is why it became the main focus of my degree and why it still captivates me.

This website collects personal projects and academic work from over the years. Not all of it is about Robotics, of course; some projects lean much more towards Art, Design and Fabrication. But the more of these projects I've worked on, the more I've noticed that skills from one domain often turn out to be unexpectedly useful in another.

That's what ties everything here together. Each project shows how I approach problems, because creative problem solving is ultimately about combining ideas, patterns and techniques, and those are usually far more similar across domains than we expect.
