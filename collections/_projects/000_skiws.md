These skis were made as part of a course at my University ("Integrative Ski Building Workshop" at ETH). They are made from a wooden core, basalt fibre mesh and epoxy resin, using an autoclave to achieve good layer adhesion. Almost all the steps were done by hand, including bending the metal edge profile. Only the step of milling the wooden cores to size was outsourced and done by a local company. The bindings were also purchased from marker and installed by myself. The skis have successfully been ridden on slopes and in powder snow!
