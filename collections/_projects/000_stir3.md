After a failed attempt at miniaturizing the Stirling engine, I opted for a slightly larger (about 20%) design. More importantly though, I again reduced friction wherever possible and planned for much tighter tolerances - which was now possible due to me having more experience with using milling machines and lathes. 

Sliding surfaces were carefully polished, the through-hole for the displacer linkage was made larger to be able to properly machine it an achieve better tolerances. Degreased ball bearings were used for all axles and the hot displacer chamber was thermally isolated better from the cool engine block to prevent the temperature from equalizing. 

As a result of all these improvements, the engine was able to run smoothly by using a small ethanol flame as a heat source!
