This engine was the main result of my high school graduation thesis. Using the insights from building the v0 prototype from household items, I was able to build a functional Stirling engine and machine all the necessary parts myself with a lathe and a milling machine. Having a much smaller displacer volume than the previous one, this model needs a propane burner as a heat source to achieve a larger temperature difference
