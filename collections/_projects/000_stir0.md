This was the first Stirling engine I ever built as part of my high school graduation thesis. Using only household items and cans it served as a perfect first experiment to get a feeling for how these engines work and which parts of the design are tricky - all while being able to quickly build different variants and try out new things. 

As this engine only runs on the heat of a small candle it was super hard to get running. It made me realize that reducing friction wherever possible and preventing air leakage are absolutely crucial to designing any functional stirling engine. Additionally, having a large displacer volume really helps with the low temperature difference of this prototype specifically.
