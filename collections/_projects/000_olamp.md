These lampshades can be folded from a single translucent sheet of plastic or paper. The number of faces, as well as their shapes can all be customized in the CAD model. The resulting flat pattern (along with a visual design) can either be printed on paper or sheets of Tyvek for water resistant lampshades. Additionally I experimented with printing very thin patterns on 3D printer using PLA filament. Printing the crease-lines slightly thinner than the rest result in a nicely foldable sheet of plastic for durable lampshades. However, PLA isn't the best material as it tends to become brittle very fast (through absorbing humidity). A next step would be to experiment with printing the patterns using Nylon - a much more flexible and durable filament.
