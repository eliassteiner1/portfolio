A couple years after having built the first real functioning stirling engine for my high school graduation thesis, I worked on a newer version; with the goal of making the entire engine much smaller. This turned out to be much harder than I initially thought. Shrinking the engine inevitably reduces the useful volume of gas inside much faster than it reduces friction causing surfaces. Additionally, manufacturing tolerances need to be much tighter than for a larger model. 

Even after countless round of remaking parts and trying to reduce friction wherever possible I was never able to get this engine to run. Ultimately, this version was abandoned in favour of a new rework and new design using all the knowledge I gained from tinkering with this version.
